## NauticMixxx {version} — RX3 Workflows, UTILITY & Beat FX

NauticMixxx {version} brings full Pioneer XDJ-RX3 interface alignment to Mixxx: unified UTILITY menu, dedicated SOURCE MY SETTINGS, Beat FX favorites with interactive X-PAD, standalone countdown timer, cached Emergency Loop playback, and seamless rekordbox USB library navigation.

### Highlights
- **Unified UTILITY**: Dedicated Deck, Mixer, Effects, Browser, and General configuration panels.
- **Beat FX & X-PAD**: 4 persistent FX favorites, continuous parameter slider, and curated FX algorithms.
- **Safety & Resilience**: Emergency Loop cached audio fail-safe, non-destructive USB sessions, and atomic settings writes.
- **Enhanced Deck Display**: Full-width playing-address warning, customizable Slip flashing, and independent per-deck Auto Cue.
